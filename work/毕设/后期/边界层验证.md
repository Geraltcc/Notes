
$$
u_{\tau}=\sqrt{{\frac{\tau_w}{\rho_w}}}
$$
$$
y^+=\frac{\rho_wu_{\tau}}{\mu_w}y
$$
$$
u^+=\frac{u_T}{u_{\tau}}
$$
对于第一层网格有
$$
u^+=u_1\sqrt{\frac{\rho_w}{\tau_w}}
$$
$$
y^+=\frac{\sqrt{\rho_w\tau_w}}{\mu_w}y_1
$$
$$
\frac{\partial u^+}{\partial y^+}=\frac{\partial \left(u_1\sqrt{\frac{\rho_w}{\tau_w}}\right)}{\partial\left(\frac{\sqrt{\rho_w\tau_w}}{\mu_w}y\right)}=\frac{\mu_w}{\tau_w}\frac{\partial u_1}{\partial y_1}
$$
于是当地的$\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}$等于
$$
\frac{\partial u_1}{\partial y_1}=\frac{\tau_w}{\mu_w}\frac{\partial u^+}{\partial y^+}
$$
因为
$$
\tau_w=(\mu+\mu_t)\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}
$$
所以
$$
\mu_t=\frac{\tau_w}{\left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}\right)}-\mu=\frac{1}{\left(\frac{\partial u^+}{\partial y^+}\right)}\mu-\mu
$$
