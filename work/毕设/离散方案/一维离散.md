# Heat Equation $\frac{\partial u}{\partial t}=\alpha \frac{\partial ^2u}{\partial x^2}$

## 1. FTCS Scheme
(forward time central space)
$ftcs:O(\Delta t,\Delta x^2)$
$$\frac{u_i^{(n+1)}-u_i^{(n)}}{\Delta t}=\alpha \frac{u_{i+1}^{(n)}-2u_{i}^{(n)}+u_{i-1}^{(n)}}{\Delta x^2}+O(\Delta x^2) $$
explicit fomula:
$$u_i^{(n+1)}=u_i^{(n)}+\frac{\alpha \Delta t}{\Delta x^2}(u_{i+1}^{(n)}-2u_{i}^{(n)}+u_{i-1}^{(n)})$$
## 2. Runge-Kutta Numerical Scheme
$rk3:O(\Delta t^3,\Delta x^2)$
$$u_i^{(1)}=u_i^{(n)}+\frac{\alpha \Delta t}{\Delta x^2}(u_{i+1}^{(n)}-2u_i^{(n)}+u_{i-1}^{(n)})\tag{1}$$
$$u_i^{(2)}=\frac{3}{4}u_i^{(n)}+\frac{1}{4}u_i^{(1)} +\frac{\alpha \Delta t}{4\Delta x^2}(u_{i+1}^{(n)}-2u_i^{(n)}+u_{i-1}^{(n)})\tag{2}$$
$$u_i^{(n+1)}=\frac{1}{3}u_i^{(n)}+\frac{2}{3}u_i^{(2)} +\frac{2\alpha \Delta t}{3\Delta x^2}(u_{i+1}^{(n)}-2u_i^{(n)}+u_{i-1}^{(n)})\tag{3}$$
## 3. Crank-Nicolson Scheme
$CK:O(\Delta t^2, \Delta x^2)$
$$\frac{u_i^{(n+1)}-u_i^{(n)}}{\Delta t}=\frac{\alpha}{2}\left[\frac{u_{i+1}^{(n)}-2u_i^{(n)}+u_{i-1}^{(n})}{\Delta x^2}+\frac{u_{i+1}^{(n+1)}-2u_i^{(n+1)}+u_{i-1}^{(n+1)}}{\Delta x^2}\right]$$
imlicit formula
三对角矩阵：
$$\beta =\frac{\alpha \Delta t}{2\Delta x^2}\tag{1}$$
$$-\beta u_{i+1}^{(n+1)}+\left(1+2\beta \right)u_i^{(n+1)}-\beta u_{i-1}^{(n+1)}=\beta u_{i+1}^{(n)}+\left(1-2\beta\right)u_i^{(n)}+\beta u_{i-1}^{(n+1)}\tag{2}$$
$$\underset{\boldsymbol{A}}{\left[\begin{array}{ccccc}b_1 & c_1 & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ a_2 & b_2 & c_2 & \cdots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & & \ddots & \vdots \\ 0 & \cdots & a_{N-1} & b_{N-1} & c_{N-1} \\ 0 & 0 & \cdots & a_N & b_N\end{array}\right]} \underset{\boldsymbol{u}}{\left[\begin{array}{c}u_1 \\ u_2 \\ \vdots \\ u_{N-1} \\ u_N\end{array}\right]}=\underset{\boldsymbol{d}}{\left[\begin{array}{c}d_1 \\ d_2 \\ \vdots \\ d_{N-1} \\ d_N\end{array}\right]}\tag{3}$$
算法：
$$\begin{array}{lr} \hline \textbf{Algorithm 1. } \text{Thomas algorithm}\\ \hline 
1. Given\quad a,b,c,d & Au=d \\ 
2. u_1=\frac{d_1}{b_1} & \text{initialise} \\
3. \textbf{for } i=2 \textbf{ to } N \textbf{ do} & \text{forward elimination}  \\
4. \qquad q_i=c_{i-1}/b_{i-1}      & \\
5. \qquad b_i=b_i-q_ia_i           & \\
6. \qquad u_i=(u_i-a_iu_{i-1})/b_i & \\
7. \textbf{end for} & \\
8. \text{for } i=N-1 \text{ to } 1 \text{do} & \text{backward substitution} \\
9. \qquad u_i=(u_i-q_{i+1}u_{i+1}) & \\
10. \textbf{end for} & \\ \hline

\end{array} $$
