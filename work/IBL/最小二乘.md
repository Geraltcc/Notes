$$
minimize \hspace{2em}J(\vec{g}) = \sum_i w_i(\vec{g}\cdot \vec{r_i} - \Delta f_i)^2
$$
加上法向正交约束惩罚项
$$
J(\vec{g}) = \sum_i w_i(\vec{g}\cdot \vec{r_i} - \Delta f_i)^2 + W_n(\vec{g} \cdot \mathbf{n} - 0)^2
$$
其中
	$\vec{g} = \nabla f = [g_x, g_y, g_z]^T$ ：待求解梯度向量
	$\vec{r_i} = \vec{x_i} - \vec{x_0} = [\Delta x_i, \Delta y_i, \Delta z_i]^T$ ：相对位置向量
	$\Delta f_i = f_i - f_0$ ：物理量差值
	$w_i$ ：邻居 $i$ 的权重
	$\mathbf{n}$ ：壁面单位法向量
	$W_n$ ：惩罚权重

推导部分：
展开目标函数:
	拟合项
$$(\vec{g}\cdot\vec{r_i} - \Delta f_i)^2=(\vec{g}\cdot \vec{r_i})^2 - 2\Delta f_i(\vec{g}\cdot \vec{r_i}) + \Delta f_i^2 = \vec{g}^T(\vec{r_i}\vec{r_i}^T)\vec{g} - 2\Delta f_i(\vec{r_i}^T\vec{g}) + \Delta f_i^2$$
	惩罚项
$$(\vec{g}\cdot \mathbf{n})^2=\vec{g}^T(\mathbf{n}\mathbf{n}^T)\vec{g}$$
	代入得
$$J(\vec{g})=\underbrace{\vec{g}^T\left(\sum_iw_i\vec{r_i}\vec{r_i}^T + W_n \mathbf{n}\mathbf{n}^T\right)\vec{g}}_{M^{\prime}} - \underbrace{2 \left(\sum_i w_i \Delta f_i \vec{r_i}\right)^T \vec{g}}_{b^T} + \underbrace{\sum_i w_i \Delta f_i^2}_{常数}$$
	对 $\vec{g}$ 求梯度：利用矩阵微分公式
	$\frac{\partial}{\partial \vec{g}}\left(\vec{g}^TA\vec{g}\right) = 2A\vec{g} \hspace{2em}当A对称$
	$\frac{\partial}{\partial \vec{g}}\left(\vec{c}^T\vec{g}\right) = \vec{c}$
	对 $J(\vec{g})$ 求导的
	$$\frac{\partial}{\partial \vec{g}} = 2M^{\prime}\vec{g} - 2 \vec{b}$$
	令导数为0
$$M^{\prime}\vec{g} = \vec{b} \hspace{2em} 3\times 3 $$关键：矩阵元素
1.原始数据矩阵 $M_0 = \sum_i w_i \vec{r_i}\vec{r_i}^T$
$$M_0 = 
\left[ 
\begin{array}{ccc} 
 \sum w_i \Delta x_i^2 & \sum w_i \Delta x_i \Delta y_i & \sum w_i \Delta x_i \Delta z_i \\
 \sum w_i \Delta y_i \Delta x_i & \sum w_i \Delta y_i^2 & \sum w_i \Delta y_i \Delta z_i \\
 \sum w_i \Delta z_i \Delta x_i & \sum w_i \Delta z_i \Delta y_i & \sum w_i \Delta z_i^2
\end{array}
\right] = 
\left[
\begin{array}{ccc}
S_{xx} & S_{xy} & S_{xz} \\
S_{yx} & S_{yy} & S_{yz} \\
S_{zx} & S_{zy} & S_{zz}
\end{array}
\right]
$$
2.惩罚项贡献 $W_n \mathbf{n}\mathbf{n}^T$
$$
W_n \mathbf{n}\mathbf{n}^T = W_n
\left[
\begin{array}{ccc}
\mathbf{n}_x^2 & \mathbf{n}_x \mathbf{n}_y & \mathbf{n_x}\mathbf{n_z} \\
\mathbf{n}_x\mathbf{n}_y & \mathbf{n}_y^2 & \mathbf{n}_y \mathbf{n}_z \\
\mathbf{n}_x \mathbf{n}_z & \mathbf{n}_y\mathbf{n}_z & \mathbf{n}_z^2
\end{array}
\right]
$$
3.组装系数矩阵 $M^{\prime} = M_0 + W_n \mathbf{n}\mathbf{n}^T$
$$
M^{\prime} = 
\left[
\begin{array}{CCC}
S_{xx} + W_n \mathbf{n}_x^2 & S_{xy} + W_n\mathbf{n}_x\mathbf{n}_y & S_{xz} + W_n \mathbf{n}_x \mathbf{n}_z \\
\cdots & S_{yy} + W_n \mathbf{n}_y^2 & S_{yz} + W_n \mathbf{n}_y\mathbf{n}_z \\
\cdots & \cdots & S_zz + W_n \mathbf{n}_z^2
\end{array}
\right]
$$
4.右端项 $\vec{b} = \sum_i w_i \Delta f_i \vec{r_i}$
$$
\vec{b} = 
\left[
\begin{array}{c}
\sum w_i \Delta f_i \Delta x_i \\ \sum w_i \Delta f_i \Delta y_i \\ \sum w_i \Delta f_i \Delta z_i
\end{array}
\right] = \left[\begin{array}{c}B_x \\ B_y \\ B_z \end{array} \right]
$$

求解方法：克莱姆法则
