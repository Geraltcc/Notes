### 一、对流项
QUICK格式
$u>0$:
$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(uk)=\frac{1}{\Delta x}\left[u_{i+1/2}\left(\frac{3}{8}k_{i+1}^n+\frac{3}{4}k_i^{n+1}-\frac{1}{8}k_{i-1}^n\right)-u_{i-1/2}\left(\frac{3}{8}k_i^{n+1}+\frac{3}{4}k_{i-1}^{n}-\frac{1}{8}k_{i-2}^{n}\right)\right]
$$
$u<0$:
$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(uk)=\frac{1}{\Delta x}\left[u_{i+1/2}\left(\frac{3}{8}k_{i}^n+\frac{3}{4}k_{i+1}^{n+1}-\frac{1}{8}k_{i+2}^n\right)-u_{i-1/2}\left(\frac{3}{8}k_{i-1}^n+\frac{3}{4}k_{i}^{n+1}-\frac{1}{8}k_{i+1}^{n}\right)\right]
$$
仅隐式当前位置点
合并写成
$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial x}(uk)=\frac{1}{\Delta x}\left[
u_{i+1/2}^+\left(\frac{3}{8}k_{i+1}^{n}+\frac{3}{4}k_{i}^{n+1}-\frac{1}{8}k_{i-1}^{n}\right)+
u_{i+1/2}^-\left(-\frac{1}{8}k_{i+2}^{n}+\frac{3}{4}k_{i+1}^{n}+\frac{3}{8}k_{i}^{n+1}\right)
\right]
$$
### 二、扩散项
$$
\frac{\partial}{\partial x}\left(\frac{\partial k}{\partial x}\right)\approx \frac{1}{\Delta x^2}\left[\Gamma_{i+1/2}(k_{i+1}^n-k_i^{n+1})-\Gamma_{i-1/2}(k_i^{n+1}-k_{i-1}^n)\right]
$$
### 三、耗散项
$$
\varepsilon^{n+1}=\varepsilon^n+\left(\frac{\partial \varepsilon}{\partial k}\right)^n(k^{n+1}-k^n)
$$
## 合并方程
$$
\left[\frac{1}{\Delta t}+\frac{3}{4\Delta x}\left(u_{i+1/2}^+-u_{i-1/2}^-\right)+\frac{\Gamma_{i+1/2}+\Gamma_{i-1/2}}{\Delta x^2}+\left(\frac{\partial \varepsilon}{\partial k}\right)^n\right]k_i^{n+1}=RHS
$$
其中$u^+=max(u,0),u^-=min(u,0)$
$$
RHS=\frac{1}{\Delta t}k_i^n+A
$$
