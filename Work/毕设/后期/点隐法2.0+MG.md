$$
\frac{\partial k}{\partial t}+\frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}(u_jk)=\frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}\left[\Gamma\frac{\partial k}{\partial x_j}\right]+P_k-\varepsilon
$$
### 一、半离散格式
$$
\frac{k^{n+1}-k^{n}}{\Delta t}
+ \frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}(u_jk)^{n+1}
= \frac{\partial}{\partial x_j}\left(\Gamma\frac{\partial k}{\partial x_j}\right)^{n+1}+P_k^n-\varepsilon^{n+1}
$$
$$
\frac{k^{n+1}-k^n}{\Delta t}+\nabla\cdot(u_jk)^{n+1}=\nabla\cdot(\Gamma\nabla k)^{n+1}+P_k^n-\varepsilon^{n+1}
$$
### 二、线性化
1. 对流项
$$
\nabla\cdot(uk)^{n+1}=\nabla\cdot(uk)^{n}+\nabla\cdot(u\Delta k)
$$
2. 扩散项
$$
\nabla\cdot(\Gamma\nabla k)^{n+1}=\nabla\cdot(\Gamma\nabla k)^{n}+\nabla\cdot[\Gamma\nabla (\Delta k)]
$$
3. 耗散项
$$
\varepsilon^{n+1}=\varepsilon^n+\left(\frac{\partial \varepsilon}{\partial k}\right)^n\Delta k
$$
组装方程
$$
\frac{\Delta k}{\Delta t}+ \nabla\cdot(u\Delta k)- \nabla\cdot[\Gamma\nabla(\Delta k)]+\left(\frac{\partial \varepsilon}{\partial k}\right)^n\Delta k=
\nabla\cdot(\Gamma\nabla k)^n- \nabla\cdot(uk)^n+ P_k^n-\varepsilon^n
$$

### 三、MultiGrid求解
写出完整离散方程
$$
\left[I+\Delta t\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla+\Delta t\left(\frac{\partial \varepsilon}{\partial k}\right)-\Delta t\nabla\cdot(\Gamma\nabla)\right]\cdot(\Delta k)=\Delta t\nabla\cdot(\Gamma\nabla k^n)-\Delta t\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla k^n+\Delta t(P_k-\varepsilon^n)
$$
或分量形式
$$
\frac{\Delta k}{\Delta t}+\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla\Delta k+\left(\frac{\partial \epsilon^n}{\partial k}\right)\Delta k-\nabla\cdot[\Gamma\nabla(\Delta k)]=\nabla\cdot(\Gamma\nabla k^n)-\mathbf{u}\cdot\nabla k^n+P_k-\varepsilon
$$
