# 笛卡尔坐标系下控制方程
## Navier-Stokes  三维  隐式离散
>$$Q=\left[\begin{array}{c}\rho \\ \rho \mathrm{u} \\ \rho \mathrm{v} \\ \rho \mathrm{w} \\ e\end{array}\right]$$
>$$F=\left[\begin{array}{c}\rho \mathrm{u} \\ \rho \mathrm{u}^2+p-\tau _{xx} \\ \rho \mathrm{u}\mathrm{v}-\tau _{xy} \\ \rho \mathrm{u}\mathrm{w}-\tau_{xz} \\ (e+p)\mathrm{u}-\mathrm{u}\tau _{xx}-\mathrm{v}\tau_{xy}-\mathrm{w}\tau_{xz}+q_x\end{array}\right]$$
>$$G=\left[\begin{array}{c}\rho \mathrm{v} \\ \rho \mathrm{u}\mathrm{v}-\tau _{xy} \\ \rho \mathrm{v}^2+p-\tau _{yy} \\ \rho \mathrm{v}\mathrm{w}-\tau_{yz} \\ (e+p)\mathrm{v}-\mathrm{u}\tau _{xy}-\mathrm{v}\tau_{yy}-\mathrm{w}\tau_{yz}+q_y\end{array}\right]$$
>$$H=\left[\begin{array}{c}\rho \mathrm{w} \\ \rho \mathrm{uw}-\tau _{xz} \\ \rho \mathrm{vw}-\tau _{yz} \\ \rho \mathrm{w}^2+p-\tau_{zz} \\ (e+p)\mathrm{w}-\mathrm{u}\tau _{xz}-\mathrm{v}\tau_{yz}-\mathrm{w}\tau_{zz}+q_z\end{array}\right]$$
>$$\tau_{xx}=\frac{2}{3}\mu \left(2\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}-\frac{\partial w}{\partial z}\right)$$
>$$\tau_{yy}=\frac{2}{3}\mu \left(2\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}-\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial w}{\partial z}\right)$$
>$$\tau_{xx}=\frac{2}{3}\mu \left(2\frac{\partial w}{\partial z}-\frac{\partial u}{\partial x}-\frac{\partial v}{\partial y}\right)$$
>$$\tau_{xy}=\mu \left(\frac{\partial u}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial v}{\partial x}\right)=\tau_{yx}$$
>$$\tau_{xz}=\mu \left(\frac{\partial w}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial u}{\partial z}\right)=\tau_{zx}$$
>$$\tau_{yz}=\mu \left(\frac{\partial v}{\partial z}+\frac{\partial w}{\partial y}\right)=\tau_{zy}$$

压力根据理想气体状态方程：
$$p=(\gamma -1)\left[e-\frac{\rho}{2}(u^2+v^2+w^2)\right]$$
$$e=\frac{p}{\gamma -1}+\frac{\rho}{2}\left(u^2+v^2+w^2\right)$$
离散方程
$$\frac{Q^{(n+1)}-Q^n}{\Delta t}+\frac{\partial F}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial G}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial H}{\partial z}=0$$

其中$F,G,H$分别为$x,y,z$方向的通量$(对流项+粘性项)$

## 近似因式分解
 1. 隐式离散与线性化
	 $$F^{(n+1)}\approx F^n+\frac{\partial F}{\partial Q}\Delta Q=F^n+A\Delta Q$$
	 其中$A=\frac{\partial F}{\partial Q}$为通量雅可比矩阵，类似地定义$B=\frac{\partial G}{\partial Q}、C=\frac{\partial H}{\partial Q}$
2. 全隐式离散方程：
$$\left[I+\Delta t\left(\frac{\partial A}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial B}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial C}{\partial z}\right)\right]\Delta Q=-\Delta t\left(\frac{\partial F^n}{\partial x}+\frac{\partial G^n}{\partial y}+\frac{\partial H^n}{\partial z}\right)$$
3. 近似隐式分解
	将多维算子分解为三个一维算子的乘积：
	$$\left[I+\Delta t\frac{\partial A}{\partial x}\right]\left[I+\Delta t\frac{\partial B}{\partial y}\right]\left[I+\Delta t\frac{\partial C}{\partial z}\right]\Delta Q=-\Delta t\cdot \mathrm{RHS}
	$$
## 逐方向求解
1. x方向扫描：
	$$\left[I+\Delta t\frac{\partial A}{\partial x}\right]\Delta Q^*=-\Delta t\cdot \mathrm{RHS}$$
	解三对角系统，更新中间变量$\Delta Q^*$
2. y方向扫描：
	$$\left[I+\Delta t\frac{\partial A}{\partial x}\right]\Delta Q^{**}=-\Delta Q^*$$
3. z方向扫描：
	$$\left[I+\Delta t\frac{\partial A}{\partial x}\right]\Delta Q=-\Delta Q^{**}$$
