# 核心方程
$$

\frac {\mathrm {d} \theta}{\mathrm {d} \xi} + (2 + H - M _ {e} ^ {2}) \frac {\theta}{u _ {e}} \frac {\mathrm {d} u _ {e}}{\mathrm {d} \xi} = \frac {C _ {f}}{2} \tag {1}
$$
$$
\theta \frac {\mathrm {d} H ^ {*}}{\mathrm {d} \xi} + [ 2 H ^ {* *} + H ^ {*} (1 - H) ] \frac {\theta}{u _ {e}} \frac {\mathrm {d} u _ {e}}{\mathrm {d} \xi} = 2 C _ {D} - H ^ {*} \frac {C _ {f}}{2} \tag {2}
$$
$$
\frac {\delta}{C _ {\tau}} \frac {\mathrm {d} C _ {\tau}}{\mathrm {d} \xi} = 4. 2 \left(C _ {\tau_ {\mathrm {E Q}}} ^ {1 / 2} - C _ {\tau} ^ {1 / 2}\right) \tag {3}
$$

## 伪时间推进法
### 方程一处理

$$
\frac{\partial \theta}{\partial t} + \vec{\mathrm{e}} \cdot \nabla_s \theta + A_1 \cdot \vec{\mathrm{e}} \cdot \nabla_s \mathrm{u_e} = S_1 \tag{1}
$$
Lax-Friedrichs修正
$$
\theta_i^{n+1} = \overline{\theta_i^n} + \Delta t\left[S_{1,i} - A_{1,i}(\vec{\mathrm{e}}\cdot \nabla_s u_e)_i - (\vec{\mathrm{e}} \cdot \nabla_s\theta)_i^n  \right]
$$
用邻居平均值替代$\theta_i^n$
$$
\overline{\theta_i^n}=(1 - \alpha)\theta_i^n + \frac{\alpha}{N_i}\sum_{j \in N_i} \theta_j^n
$$
等价于隐式数值扩散，扩散系数 $\approx \frac{\alpha \Delta^2}{2 \Delta t}$
稳定性条件 CFL:
$$
\Delta t < \frac{\Delta}{|\vec{\mathrm{e}} \cdot \nabla_s|} \approx \frac{\Delta}{1} = \Delta
$$
因为 $\vec{\mathrm{e}}$ 是单位向量，特征速度量级为 $O(1)$


## !!!完整展开得到——方程一!!!
$$
\theta_i^{n+1} = (1 - \alpha)\theta_i^n + \frac{\alpha}{N_i}\sum_{j \in N_i} \theta_j^n + \Delta t \left[\frac{C_{f,i}^{n}}{2} - \left((2 + H_i^n - (M_{e, i}^n)^2)\frac{\theta_i^n}{u_{e, i}^n}\right)(\vec{\mathrm{e_i}} \cdot \nabla_s u_e)_i^n - (\vec{\mathrm{e_i}} \cdot \nabla_s \theta)_i^n \right]
$$
### 方程二处理
$$
\theta \frac {\mathrm {d} H ^ {*}}{\mathrm {d} \xi} + [ 2 H ^ {* *} + H ^ {*} (1 - H) ] \frac {\theta}{u _ {e}} \frac {\mathrm {d} u _ {e}}{\mathrm {d} \xi} = 2 C _ {D} - H ^ {*} \frac {C _ {f}}{2} \tag {2}
$$
两边除以 $\theta$ 并添加伪时间项
$$
\frac{\partial H^*}{\partial t} + \vec{\mathrm{e}} \cdot \nabla_s H^* + A_2 (\vec{\mathrm{e}} \cdot \nabla_s u_e) = S_2 
$$
其中
$$
A_2 = \frac{2H^{**} + H^*(1 - H)}{u_e}
$$
$$
S_2 = \frac{1}{\theta}\left(2C_D - H^* \frac{C_f}{2}\right)
$$

## !!!完整展开得到——方程二!!!
$$
{H_i^*}^{n+1} = (1 - \alpha){H_i^*}^n + \frac{\alpha}{N_i}\sum_{j \in N_i} \theta_j^n + \Delta t 
\left[ 
\frac{1}{\theta_i^n}\left(2C_{D, i}^n - {H_i^*}^n\frac{C_{f, i}^n}{2}\right) - \left( \frac{2{H_i^{**}}^n + {H_i^*}^n(1 - H_i^n)}{u_{e, i}^n}\right)(\vec{\mathrm{e} \cdot \nabla_s u_e})_i^n - (\vec{\mathrm{e}} \cdot \nabla_s H^*)_i^n
\right]
$$

### 方程三处理
$$
\frac {\delta}{C _ {\tau}} \frac {\mathrm {d} C _ {\tau}}{\mathrm {d} \xi} = 4. 2 \left(C _ {\tau_ {\mathrm {E Q}}} ^ {1 / 2} - C _ {\tau} ^ {1 / 2}\right) \tag {3}
$$
引入伪时间项，并且根据 $\delta \approx \theta \cdot H$ (位移厚度关系)
$$
\frac{\partial C_\tau}{\partial t} + \vec{\mathrm{e}} \cdot \nabla_s C_\tau = S_3
$$
其中
$$
S_3 = \frac{4.2C_\tau}{\theta H}\left(C_{\tau, eq}^{\frac{1}{2}} - C_\tau^{\frac{1}{2}}\right)
$$
## !!!完整展开得到——方程三!!! （可以不用粘性项）
$$
C_{\tau ,i}^{n+1} = (1 - \alpha)C_{\tau, i}^n
 + \frac{\alpha}{N_i} \sum_{j \in N_i}C_{\tau, j}^n + \Delta t 
 \left[
 4.2\frac{C_{\tau,i}^n}{\theta_i^n H_i^n}\left({C_{\tau, eq, i}^n}^{\frac{1}{2}} - {C_{\tau, i}^n}^{\frac{1}{2}}\right) - (\vec{\mathrm{e}}\cdot \nabla_s C_\tau)_i^n
 \right]
$$

## 封闭关系式
$C_f$ 公式
$$
F_c = (1 + 0.2 M_e^2)^{\frac{1}{2}}
$$
$$
F_c C_f = 0.3e^{-1.33H_k}\left[log_{10}\left(\frac{Re_\theta}{F_c}\right)\right]^{-1.74-0.31H_k}
$$
$H^*$ 公式
$$
H_0 = 
\begin{cases}
4, & Re_\theta < 400 \\
3 + \frac{400}{Re_\theta}, & Re_\theta > 400
\end{cases}
$$

$$
H^* = 
\begin{cases}
1.505 + \frac{4}{Re_\theta} + \left(0.165 - \frac{1.6}{Re_\theta^{\frac{1}{2}}}\right)\frac{(H_0 - H_k)^{1.6}}{H_k}, & H_k < H_0 \\
1.505 + \frac{4}{Re_\theta} + (H_k - H_0)^2\left[\frac{0.04}{H_k} + \frac{0.007log(Re_\theta)}{(H_k - H_0 +\frac{4}{log(Re_\theta)})^2}\right], & H_k > H_0
\end{cases}
$$
$C_D$ 公式
$$
U_s = \frac{H^*}{2}(1 - \frac{4}{3}\frac{H_k - 1}{H})
$$
$$
C_D = \frac{C_f}{2}U_s + C_\tau(1 - U_s)
$$
$C_{\tau, eq}$ 公式
$$
\delta = \theta\left(3.15 + \frac{1.72}{H_k - 1}\right) + \delta^*
$$
$$
C_{\tau,eq} = H^*\frac{0.015}{1 - U_s}\frac{(H_k - 1)^3}{H_k^2H}
$$
